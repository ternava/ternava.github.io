Research projects & grants

  • deTOG (Feature Toggle Removal: Automation, Testing Foundations, and Measuring the Gains). ANR JCJC 2026 research project (2027–2031), of which I am the Principal Investigator (PI) at Télécom Paris. deTOG will investigate automated, safe removal of stale feature toggles to reduce software bloat and shrink the attack surface of modern CI/CD codebases.
